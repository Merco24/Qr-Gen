Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Owntools.

Is Owntools really free?

Yes, absolutely. Owntools is a passion project built to provide robust tools without subscriptions or hidden fees. We utilize open-source models and free APIs to keep it accessible for everyone.

How does the Background Remover work without sending my data to a server?

We use cutting-edge WebAssembly AI models (@imgly/background-removal) that download and run entirely within your browser. Your images never leave your device, ensuring 100% privacy and no server costs.

Do I need to create an account?

No account creation is required. You can start using all the tools immediately upon visiting the site. No emails, no passwords, just productivity.
